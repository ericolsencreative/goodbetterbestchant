Every game ball Ben Johnson has handed out

Who got one, in which game, and why. Updated after every Bears win.

Last updated November 10, 2026

40 game balls to 28 different recipients across 20 wins, through Week 9 of 2026. Cairo Santos and Caleb Williams lead with 4 each.

After every win, Ben Johnson hands out game balls in the locker room before he leads the Good, Better, Best chant. Nobody keeps the list in one place, so this page does. Every entry links to the report it came from and to that game's page, where you can watch the room.

The ones that went back to the coach

Twice, Caleb Williams has turned around and handed a ball to Johnson. Those are not counted above.

  • Week 3 vs Cowboys: Caleb Williams tossed the last ball back to Johnson after his first regular-season win as head coach. source
  • Wild Card vs Packers: Williams gave Johnson a ball for his first playoff win, calling the coach "monumental" in his life. source

How this list is kept

A ball is counted when a named outlet or the team itself reported it. Where a ball went to a group, such as the offensive line, it counts once. Where nothing was reported for a win, the row says so instead of guessing. The list is updated after every Bears win. Spot a mistake or a missing ball? See the about page for how to reach us.

FAQ

Who has the most game balls under Ben Johnson?

Cairo Santos and Caleb Williams lead with 4 each through Week 9 of the 2026 season. D'Andre Swift has 3; D'Marco Jackson has 3; Colston Loveland has 2; DJ Moore has 2.

How many game balls has Ben Johnson given out?

40 that have been reported, to 28 different recipients across 20 wins since August 2025.

Who got the game ball in the Bears' last win?

Jaylon Johnson, after the 27–24 win over Tampa Bay Buccaneers on November 8, 2026.

Has Ben Johnson given a game ball to a coach?

Yes. Defensive coordinator Dennis Allen got the only ball after the win over New Orleans, his former team, in October 2025, and shared one with the whole defense after the Week 3 win over Philadelphia in 2026. Team president Kevin Warren also got one in Minnesota.

When does Ben Johnson hand out game balls?

In the locker room right after a win, before he leads the Good, Better, Best chant. Recipients often say a few words to the team.
