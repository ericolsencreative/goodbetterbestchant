FAQ

How many times have the Bears done the good, better, best chant?

19 times through Week 8 of the 2026 season: 13 in 2025 (including preseason and the wild-card win) and 6 so far in 2026.

Do the Bears do the chant after every game?

Only after wins. There is no chant after a loss.

When was the first good, better, best chant?

August 17, 2025, after a 38–0 preseason win over Buffalo. The first one fans saw was September 21, 2025, after beating Dallas.

Did the Bears chant after the playoff win over Green Bay?

Yes. January 10, 2026, after coming back from 21–3 down to win 31–27, their first playoff win since January 2011.
