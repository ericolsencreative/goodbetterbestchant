The 2025 season, chant by chant

Ben Johnson's first year: 13 chants, from a preseason shutout to a playoff comeback.

Last updated November 10, 2026

13 wins, 13 chants in 2025: 8 at home and 5 on the road. 8 were decided by one score. In the 11 regular-season wins the Bears outscored opponents 305–217.

This was Ben Johnson's first season as head coach, and the season the chant went from a locker-room ritual to something the whole city yelled. It started after a preseason shutout of Buffalo, went public after the Week 3 win over Dallas, and peaked with the shirtless chant in Philadelphia and the Wild Card comeback over Green Bay, the Bears' first playoff win since January 2011. The regular season finished 11–6.
